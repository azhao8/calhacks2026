# Safety resources (draft, needs a human check)

For "Need help?" on meetup day (S13) and "Safety resources" in the menu (S16). Melos is not an emergency service and never implies it watches meetups.

**Status: not yet verified.** Every number below came from search results that quote the official page. The research session couldn't open the pages themselves, because its network blocks berkeley.edu, berkeleyca.gov, and oaklandca.gov. Before real students see these, someone opens each link once on a phone and checks the number and hours. Then they write their name and the date in the last column. Show only checked rows. Until then the screens show 911 only.

Phone numbers are written with spaces (no dashes, per the copy rules) and linked with `tel:`.

## Notes from the research

- BearWALK is now called Safewalk, and booking moved to the "UC Berkeley Safewalk" app. Older pages still say BearWALK and list a 3 am close.
- Safewalk covers only the area bounded by Vine, Milvia, Derby, and Prospect. It doesn't help at Oakland venues.
- Two UHS pages show 855 817 8667, which looks like a typo for 855 817 5667. Check this one on the page itself.
- Left out on purpose: Berkeley's Specialized Care Unit (city pages give three different sets of hours), and UCPD's CalTIP text line (UCPD pages disagree on the number).
